Who We Are

Built by Engineers. Delivered by Contractors.

OGC Industries was built to bring engineering-level rigor into commercial electrical contracting. We combine code-driven design thinking, field-ready documentation, and contractor accountability under one roof.

Our team works across commercial construction, high-rise MEP coordination, renewable energy systems, and installation execution. That means clients get solutions that are technically sound, constructible, and ready to stand up under AHJ review.

Whether the project calls for a full electrical design package, a solar interconnection study, or commercial installation support, OGC Industries delivers with precision, coordination, and follow-through.
