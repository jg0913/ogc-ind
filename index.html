Who We Are

About OGC Industries

OGC Industries is an electrical contracting and MEP engineering consulting firm founded by Jonathan Ochoa Guzman — a licensed electrical engineer with hands-on experience across commercial construction, high-rise MEP coordination, and renewable energy systems.
