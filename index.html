About › Safety
Safety Program

Our Safety Commitment

Electrical work carries real risk. OGC Industries treats safety as a core operational discipline — not a checkbox. Our comprehensive safety program meets or exceeds OSHA, NFPA 70E, and NEC requirements on every project, every day.

OSHA 30 Compliance

All crew leads hold current OSHA 30-hour certifications. Site safety plans are prepared before mobilization on every project.

Arc Flash Program

We conduct arc-flash hazard analyses on all energized work per NFPA 70E. Appropriate PPE is mandatory — no exceptions.

Daily Toolbox Talks

Every crew starts the day with a safety briefing covering task-specific hazards, weather conditions, and emergency procedures.

Lockout / Tagout

Strict LOTO procedures are enforced on all isolation and de-energization tasks. Verification is required before any work begins.

Incident Reporting

We maintain a near-miss reporting culture. Every incident — however minor — is documented, reviewed, and used to improve procedures.

Client Site Programs

We integrate seamlessly with owner safety programs and can provide project-specific safety plans, JSAs, and activity hazard analyses on request.
